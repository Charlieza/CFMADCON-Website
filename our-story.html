CFMADCON / Our story

Founded where finance meets science.

CFMADCON was founded by a tax specialist and an astrophysicist. One brought the structure and rigour of financial practice; the other, the systems thinking and data skills of science. That combination still defines how we work: every engagement is evidence-based, well documented and built to last.

Our identity

Two heritages, one firm.

Our pattern is an original CFMADCON design inspired by the Venda and Swati heritage of our two founders. Fine stripes, a plait and dotted binding are inspired by Venda minwenda cloth; a zigzag, an oval chain, a striped frame and a key border are inspired by Swati emahiya cloth. Lines of CFMADCON berry, the colour of our logo, hold them together.

Our mark, a halftone globe with an orbit, reflects the other side of the firm: data, science and a view of Earth from space.
