Three practices. One standard of quality.

Practice 01

Finance & Tax

We help businesses, trusts and public benefit organisations keep accurate financial records, meet their tax obligations and resolve matters with SARS.

Core services

  • Accounting and bookkeeping
  • Tax compliance, including returns, PAYE, VAT and customs
  • SARS disputes and Smart Tax Advisory

How we work with you

  • Once-off support: tax returns, verifications and disputes
  • Monthly retainers for ongoing accounting, compliance and monitoring
  • Registered representative updates, IT3(t), IT3(d) and other third-party returns
  • A diagnostic first when records are behind, then a structured catch-up

Practice 02

Data & Impact

We help funded organisations measure, evidence and report the impact of their work.

Core services

  • Monitoring and evaluation (M&E) systems and impact frameworks
  • Grant management, Section 18A and PBO compliance
  • Dashboards, forecasting and data audits

Where we help

  • An impact framework and indicators for a new funded programme
  • Grant reporting packs that combine financial and impact data
  • Section 18A and PBO compliance reviews
  • Dashboards that track delivery against funder requirements

Practice 03

Space & Earth Observation

We apply satellite data and space-sector expertise to environmental risk, disclosure and programme design.

Core services

  • EagleNatureInsight™ nature-related risk assessment
  • Earth observation and geospatial analytics
  • Space-sector advisory: programme design, funding and M&E

What we bring

  • Satellite and environmental data processed for you, with no GIS team needed
  • Site-level evidence aligned with the TNFD LEAP approach
  • Programme design, funding strategy and M&E for space programmes
  • EagleNatureInsight™ assessments delivered with Space Eagle
