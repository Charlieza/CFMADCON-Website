Why it matters

Nature is now a business question.

Financiers, buyers and investors increasingly expect companies to understand their dependencies and impacts on nature. The Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) provides the framework many of them now use.

EagleNatureInsight turns that framework into something a small or medium-sized business can act on: a clear view of each site, what the evidence says, and what to do next.

How it works

Built around the TNFD LEAP approach.

Choose your business activity and the places that matter, draw a site boundary or upload your area, then explore the satellite view and combine environmental data with your own records and field observations.

  1. L · 01

    Locate

    Locate your interface with nature.

    Map your sites and the surrounding landscape, including water systems, ecosystems and biodiversity areas.

  2. E · 02

    Evaluate

    Evaluate dependencies and impacts.

    Link your business activities to the natural systems they use and affect, adding your own evidence.

  3. A · 03

    Assess

    Assess risks and opportunities.

    Review what measured conditions could mean for production, costs and continuity, and prioritise the issues.

  4. P · 04

    Prepare

    Prepare to respond and report.

    Turn the findings into an action plan and a report you can share.

The evidence

Satellite data, explained for business.

  • Water, vegetation, land cover, climate hazard and biodiversity context indicators
  • Satellite (Earth observation) imagery of your sites and their surroundings
  • Regional species and plant records, recent vegetation and weather, and long-term climate scenarios
  • Your own water, land-use and operational records, field photos and observations

New datasets are added as their coverage, access and relevance are established. You do not need your own satellite data or GIS team.
